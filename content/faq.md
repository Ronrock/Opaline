faqs:
  - q: How do I get to Villa Opaline from Corfu Airport?
    a: "The villa is approximately 25–30 minutes by car (around 22 km via the coastal road). We recommend hiring a car — the island's beauty is best explored at your own pace. Taxis are readily available at the airport, or we can arrange a private transfer in advance. Please contact us at least 48 hours before arrival."
  - q: What are the check-in and check-out times?
    a: "Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. If you require an early check-in or late check-out, please let us know in advance — we will do our utmost to accommodate you, subject to availability."
  - q: Is there a minimum stay requirement?
    a: "During high season (July and August), a minimum stay of 7 nights is required. During shoulder season (May, June, and September), the minimum is 4 nights. For off-season stays, shorter periods may be available — please enquire directly."
  - q: Is Villa Opaline suitable for families with young children?
    a: "The villa is warmly welcoming to families. A travel cot and highchair can be provided upon request. Please note that the pool area is open and unfenced, so children must be supervised by an adult at all times. The terraced gardens also have some steps and uneven surfaces."
  - q: Is WiFi available throughout the villa?
    a: "Yes, high-speed fibre broadband WiFi is available throughout all indoor spaces and across the pool terrace, complimentary for all guests."
  - q: Can we arrange a private chef or additional services?
    a: "Absolutely. We offer a curated concierge service including private chef bookings, in-villa massage and beauty treatments, grocery delivery, private boat charters, and car hire. Please submit requests at least 48 hours in advance and we will arrange everything for you."
  - q: Are bed linens and towels provided?
    a: "Yes. All beds are made up with luxury Egyptian cotton linens, and bathroom towels are provided for all guests. Pool towels are also available. Linens are changed every third day during your stay; towels may be exchanged daily on request."
  - q: Is there air conditioning?
    a: "All bedrooms and the main living area are equipped with individual air conditioning units. We ask that you keep windows and doors closed while air conditioning is running, and switch units off when leaving a room."
  - q: What is the security deposit policy?
    a: "A refundable security deposit is required at the time of booking. This is returned in full within 7 days of check-out, provided no damage or additional cleaning beyond normal use is required. Full details are included in the rental agreement."
  - q: Is outdoor dining possible at the villa?
    a: "Yes — the main terrace features a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen with a gas barbecue and a shaded dining area for up to 8 guests. The pool terrace also has sun loungers and a small al fresco cocktail area. These are among our guests' favourite features of the villa."
