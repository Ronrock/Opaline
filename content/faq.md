    a: "We provide self check-in and check-out, the detailed process will be shared before the check-in day"

    a: "A minimum stay of 5 nights is required, shorter periods may be available outside of the high season — please enquire directly."
  - q: Is Villa Opaline suitable for families with young children?
    a: "The villa is warmly welcoming to families. Please note that the pool area is open and unfenced, so children must be supervised by an adult at all times. Absolutely no walking on the infinity part of the pool because of falling danger."
  - q: Is the water safe to drink?
    a: "We advice against drinking the tap water and only drink bottled water. The local water is highly mineralized and hard. While safe, the unconventional taste and mineral load frequently cause stomach upset for visitors. The minerals can also leave calcium stains on the dishes after using the dishwasher, this is not harmful. To prevent this, rinse the cutlery and dishes well before putting them in the dishwasher."   
  - q: Is WiFi available throughout the villa?
    a: "Yes, high-speed fibre broadband WiFi is available throughout all indoor spaces, details will be provided before check-in day"
  - q: Are bed linens and towels provided?
    a: "Yes. All beds are made up with linens, and bathroom towels are provided for all guests. Pool towels are also available."
  - q: Is there air conditioning?
    a: "All bedrooms and the main living area are equipped with individual air conditioning units. We ask that you keep windows and doors closed while air conditioning is running, and switch units off when leaving a room."
  - q: Is outdoor dining possible at the villa?
    a: "Yes — the main terrace has a shaded dining area with a gas barbecue."
  - q: How does the gas barbecue work?
    a: "Please make sure that before use you open the main valve for the gas (in the cupboard below the barbecue) and close it after use"
